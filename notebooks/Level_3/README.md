# Level 3: Advanced Classification, Dashboards & Sentiment Analysis

The final advanced tier focusing on predictive classification for customer churn, interactive business intelligence dashboards, and natural language text processing.

## Tasks Covered:
1. **Task 1: Classification Models (`Level_3_Task_1_Classification.ipynb`)**
   - Training and tuning Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, and Random Forest classifiers.
   - Using datasets like `telecom_churn_master.csv`, `churn-bigml-80.csv`, and `churn-bigml-20.csv`.
   - Evaluation metrics: Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, and Confusion Matrix.

2. **Task 2: Dashboarding (`Level_3_Task_2_Dashboard.ipynb` & `Dashboard.pbix`)**
   - Integrating analytical outputs with Power BI dashboards.
   - Dynamic filtering and KPI visualization for business stakeholders.

3. **Task 3: Sentiment Analysis (`Level_3_Task_3_Sentiment_Analysis.ipynb`)**
   - Text preprocessing and tokenization.
   - Polarity scoring and sentiment classification patterns.
